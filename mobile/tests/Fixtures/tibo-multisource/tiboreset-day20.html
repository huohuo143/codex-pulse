When does your own Codex usage reset?

Global resets are rare; your own weekly window, and the 5-hour one if your plan has it, refill on a clock of their own. Paste /status into the usage clock to see your countdowns, and check whether the last Tibo reset was worth anything to you.

Questions people ask

It is the community's name for a global Codex usage reset announced by Tibo on X. When one lands, every eligible account is refilled at once, whatever was left: the weekly limit, and the 5-hour limit on plans that have one. It is a goodwill gesture rather than a scheduled event, so the timing is never guaranteed.

A global reset refills the usage limits of every eligible account at once, with nothing for you to do. A banked reset only drops a credit into your account: your usage stays exactly where it was until you redeem that credit yourself, and an unredeemed one can expire. The tracker shows the latest of each.

Mostly, yes. People say "ChatGPT reset" because Codex usage comes with a ChatGPT plan, and the resets Tibo announces refill exactly those Codex limits in the app, the CLI and the IDE extension. Ordinary ChatGPT chat limits keep their own clocks, so a Codex reset does not touch your chat allowance.
